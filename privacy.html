OC-Rook privacy policy

Updated 14 November 2026.

OC-Rook is used only by its owner. When the owner signs in with Google, OC-Rook gets access to that Google account only within the permissions shown on the Google consent screen.

The data stays on the owner's own computers and in the owner's own Google account. OC-Rook does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone and does not use it for advertising.

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions: use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for OC-Rook.
